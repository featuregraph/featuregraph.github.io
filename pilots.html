Analysis pilots

An analysis built on your data, checked and delivered.

A pilot is a fixed-fee engagement. We set up FeatureGraph on a historical extract of your scientific or engineering time-series data and answer an agreed set of questions. Your team gets the results, and a record of how each one was produced.

What you get

Results you can check without reading code.

  • A report written for your domain experts: the answer to each agreed question, figures of your signals with the detected objects marked, the parameters we chose and why, and what the results do and do not show.
  • Result tables in CSV files, so you can use the measurements in your own tools.
  • A provenance record for every result, so each one can be reproduced from the same data and the same definitions.

Every analysis is run through the FeatureGraph compiler, which checks it before it executes. Running it uses no language model.

How it works

From first conversation to results.

  1. Fit call. A short call to confirm your data suits FeatureGraph and the question can be answered. If it does not, we say so.
  2. Written scope and fixed fee. The questions, the dataset, what “done” means, and the price, on one page.
  3. Agreement. A confidentiality agreement and written pilot terms, including how your data and results are handled.
  4. Setup and analysis. You send one data extract. We set up the data, choose the processing parameters, build the objects and answer the agreed questions.
  5. Delivery and review. We walk your team through the report and the record.
  6. Decision. You continue with FeatureGraph, or you stop and keep the report.

A pilot takes three to four weeks from the day we receive your data. The fee is fixed and agreed before work begins.

What we need

One data extract and a few hours of an expert’s time.

  • An extract of the historical signals to analyze, with timestamps, units and sampling rate, plus any events or labels you trust.
  • A short description of the data: what each column is and any known problems such as gaps or calibration changes.
  • A point of contact who can answer questions about the data during setup.
  • A review of how we have mapped your data, to confirm that the objects match what you mean by an event.

The current version is built for oscillating signals, such as respiration, pressure changes in industrial equipment, or mechanical pulses from a rotating drill string.

Your data

Handled by written agreement.

FeatureGraph does not retain your raw data after the pilot. The terms for your data and for the results are agreed in writing before work starts. You keep your data and your results.

Not included

What a pilot does not cover.

  • A live connection to your data warehouse or database.
  • Production deployment.
  • Clinical or medical interpretation of results.

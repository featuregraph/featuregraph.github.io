FeatureGraph keeps signals, states, events, object boundaries, summaries, and disagreements available for inspection.

Directional states

Explicit rising and non-rising regions produced by a declared lag, threshold, and state-gap rule.

Candidate oscillations

Trough, peak, and end boundaries with one identifier per candidate object. The construction does not decide whether a candidate is physiologically meaningful.

Object tables

One row per object with boundaries, period, excursion, symmetry, completeness, and parent relationships.

Accumulation curves

Waveform contribution relative to an explicit baseline, integrated within a parent object and measured through area, rates, centroid, and timing.

Roadmap

Preserve the evidence, then simplify the architecture.

The released beta remains active and public-facing. Its BIDMC study is a durable record of what explicit oscillation objects preserved and where their detector failed. The next research line begins from transitions rather than assuming oscillations.

Completed research pass

BIDMC LLM-preservation study.

Complete

Independent baseline

An LLM analyzed raw BIDMC respiration without receiving FeatureGraph output.

Complete

Object-level comparison

Individual candidate breaths were matched, exposing boundary agreement and unmatched objects instead of relying on aggregate means.

Complete

Self-implemented detector

The beta records explicit lag, threshold, state-gap, peak-boundary, and incomplete-object behavior without native SciPy peak finding.

Complete

Multi-subject transfer

Fixed and MAD-scaled rules were run across BIDMC subjects. The negative transfer result is retained rather than hidden by further tuning.

Complete

Accumulation demonstration

Wave-relative area was represented as a dependent object with parent identity, completeness, timing, rates, and centroid.

Complete

Research manuscript

A working paper documents the successful preservation result, definition mismatches, and detector failure.

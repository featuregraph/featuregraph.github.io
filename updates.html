Research Updates

A public record of how FeatureGraph is evolving.

These updates record positive and negative results as the project develops explicit, inspectable behavioral representations.

August 2026

CLaP detector output becomes explicit FeatureGraph objects ↗

The public from_state_sequence adapter materialized 20,700 unchanged CLaP labels as nine bounded objects and eight temporal relations. All eleven study checks passed, including exact reconstruction of the original state sequence.

August 2026

BIDMC study reproduces a reference analysis—and exposes a transfer failure ↗

On subject 1, all 169 reference candidate breaths matched FeatureGraph results. Across subjects, the same fixed rule over-segmented, and robust MAD scaling increased recall while producing still more extra candidates. The study supports FeatureGraph as a deterministic preservation and audit layer, not an autonomous judge of meaningful peaks.

August 2026

Respiration accumulation as a dependent behavioral object

Waveform area relative to an explicit baseline is recorded with parent identity, completeness, timing, rates, and centroid. It is not interpreted as physical inhaled volume without sensor calibration.

July 2026

Transition objects become the compositional layer ↗

Rising, falling, and inactive behavior is represented as explicit Transition objects. This development line now motivates the transition-only successor study.

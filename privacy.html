Privacy

How this site measures visits

featuregraph.ai uses PostHog to understand which pages are read and how people find them.

What is collected

Pages viewed, links and buttons clicked, how far a page is scrolled, the referring site, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and your browser and device type.

Visits may also be recorded as a session replay: a reconstruction of page activity such as scrolling and mouse movement, used to see how pages are read. Anything typed into a form is masked and never recorded.

What it is used for

Only to improve this site and to understand who is reading FeatureGraph's research. The data is not sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other sources. Session replays are kept for 30 days.

Opting out

A content blocker or your browser's tracking protection will stop PostHog from loading, and the site works the same without it.

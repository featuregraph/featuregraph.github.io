Completed interoperability study

CLaP states as explicit FeatureGraph occurrence objects

CLaP detected three recurring states in the Crop benchmark. The public FeatureGraph API materialized all 20,700 unchanged labels as nine bounded objects and eight relations, then reconstructed the source sequence exactly.

Preprint

BIDMC multiscale study, preprint draft

Trough-peak-trough objects constructed at rolling-envelope scales of 79 and 100 samples on all 53 BIDMC records, under a contract frozen after four development records. On 20 eligible held-out records the shorter-scale-only objects sit at more consistent cardiac phase, a median difference of 0.269, positive in 14 of 20. Five figures regenerate from committed tables.
